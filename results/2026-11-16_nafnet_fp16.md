# NAFNet fp16 NaN: root cause and fix (2026-11-16)

Base: origin/work/ports ae0ba4c4 + this change. GPU: RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB. Raw rows: `results/raw/nafnet_fp16_s16/`, `results/raw/nafnet_fp16_s1024/`.

## Root cause
The runner scales the residual stream by `_STREAM` (exact power of two, norm eps scaled by `_STREAM^2`). With `_STREAM = 1/16` the deepest blocks' `conv3` output reached ~34368 (scaled), ~5.5e5 true scale, on low-contrast noise. `inp + conv3` overflowed fp16 to inf, the next norm gave NaN at `encoders.3.13.conv4`.

## Fix
`_STREAM = 1/1024` (gate scale `1/64` unchanged). Norms are scale-free and fp16 keeps relative precision, so results are unchanged. Applies to every NAFNet-type model (gopro, sidd; same runner).

## Stress inputs (fp16, nafnet-gopro, portable and kernels)
| input | before (1/16) | after (1/1024) |
|---|---|---|
| synthetic, noise10, noise_full | NaN | finite (mean abs diff vs fp32 0.001-0.002) |
| const, binary, checker, noise2, tiny32, real GoPro crops | finite | finite |
nafnet-sidd was finite before and after. 1/256, 1/4096, 1/8192 also tried; 1/1024 chosen.

gopro (56 frames) PSNR 33.32 / SSIM 0.9413; sidd (64 crops) PSNR 40.32 / SSIM 0.9252; portable and kernels.
